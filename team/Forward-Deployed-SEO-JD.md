## About us

We build Berlin — an AI agent that automates Search Visibility end-to-end. Modern search visibility (SEO + AEO + GEO) breaks down into three pillars: **strategy, execution, and the tools that power both.** Berlin automates across all three. The kind of work that traditionally takes an SEO team weeks gets compressed into hours.

We're now hiring our first **SEO Strategist** to own client accounts, drive real outcomes across SEO and AI search, and make Berlin sing in the wild.

## About the role

This is a hands-on, client-facing role — the kind of work an experienced in-house SEO or agency-side SEO does — with two big differences: Berlin handles the execution grunt work alongside you, and **you'll use Berlin exclusively** for that execution. You bring the strategy, the judgment, the client ownership, and the experimental nose for what's working in modern search. Berlin ships.

It's a **pure delivery role** — no sales, no pitching new business. Your job is to drive results for the clients you own across SEO and AI search, and to build the playbooks the rest of the team will inherit.

**Velocity is the job.** We are not a typical agency. We don't ship a fixed list of deliverables every month. Search visibility is moving faster than wildfire — AI overviews, LLM citations, answer engines, new ranking signals every quarter — and the only way to keep up is to run fast. We apply things fast, we verify experiments fast, we kill what doesn't work fast, and we do dramatically more for each client than a traditional agency would in the same window. Berlin is what makes that pace possible.

As the first SEO hire on the delivery side, you'll work directly with the founders and have a direct line into Berlin's roadmap. When you hit something Berlin can't do yet, you tell us, and it gets built.

## What you'll do

- Own search visibility end-to-end for clients across ecom, SaaS, and local / professional services — outcomes, not a fixed deliverable list
- Lead the application of Berlin for each client: research what's working *in this client's context*, design the experiments, run them through Berlin, read the results, double down or kill
- Run a continuous experimentation loop across SEO, AEO, GEO, and whatever new surface shows up next quarter. There is no "we don't do that here" — if it might move the needle, you try it
- Build segment-specific playbooks — because ecom, SaaS, and local search are three different sports
- Run **weekly client calls (minimum)** to align on what you're testing, what's landed, and what's next
- Read relentlessly — SEO research, operator Twitter, newsletters, Reddit, AI search papers — and translate what's actually working *today* into client work within days, not quarters
- Feed the product team continuous feedback: where Berlin nailed it, where it missed, what features would 10× your throughput
- Document everything — client decisions, experiment results, Berlin prompts and workflows — so the playbook compounds

## Who you are

- **2–4 years of hands-on SEO** with results you can point to — traffic growth, rankings, revenue impact. Real numbers, not vanity metrics.
- A **generalist**, not a pure specialist in one lane. You've done technical, on-page, content, and off-page work.
- You've worked with **ecom, SaaS, or local / professional services** clients — ideally more than one segment.
- You've been forced to **rethink what "good SEO" means in the last 18 months**. AI overviews, LLM citations, answer engines — you've run experiments, watched others run them, or have strong opinions you can back up.
- **Fluent with AI tools** in your daily workflow. You've built your own GPT / Claude / Perplexity workflows for SEO. Maybe you've written scripts. You don't need convincing that AI changes how this job gets done.
- **You treat speed of iteration as the core skill.** Shipping ten experiments and reading the results sounds more fun to you than perfecting one.
- **Comfortable owning client outcomes solo.** No team to hide behind. Comfortable running weekly client calls and driving the agenda.
- You **write well** — playbooks, experiment write-ups, client updates that people actually read.

## Who you are NOT

- Someone whose best SEO work is 3+ years old. SEO has changed. If your playbook is still 2020-era, this isn't the role.
- A pure strategist who doesn't ship. This role is execution-heavy and runs at experiment-a-week (or faster) cadence.
- Allergic to giving blunt product feedback. We want you to complain loudly — and constructively — when Berlin gets something wrong.
- An agency veteran looking for a senior oversight role. You're the one doing the work.
