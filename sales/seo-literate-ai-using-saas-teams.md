# ICP 1 — SEO-Literate, AI-Using SaaS Teams & Founders

* **Vertical:** SaaS

* **Revenue:** 80k - 200k MRR (800k - 25 million ARR)

* **Active SEO:** Already doing real SEO work — not starting from zero.

* **AI usage:** Already using AI tools like claude to do the SEO work or marketing work

* **Team shape:** A small SEO/marketing team, or a founder doing the SEO themselves

* **Buyer literacy:** The owner (founder or marketing leader) personally understands SEO/AEO well — a literate buyer who can operate a platform

* **Filtering Prompt:** We can check the number of pages they have to understand this. If they have 500+ they are investing a lot on SEO. Our reference - scale.jobs has around 2k pages. 200 to 500 pages website is also investing in SEO. Also check the existence of llms.txt file.
