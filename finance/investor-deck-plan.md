## Slide 6 — Flagship Case (Substrate Proof)

**Eyebrow:** SUBSTRATE PROOF
**Headline:** 2.5M pages audited. 12,000 broken URLs classified. 15 hours.
**Subhead:** The engineering substrate working in practice — the reason one FDM ships agency-scoped outcomes at agency-fraction cost.

**The story:**
An e-commerce operator running a **2.5 million page catalogue across 6 countries** had **12,000 soft 404s** — pages returning a 200 status but serving nothing. Redirects had to stay within each country's site. Berlin's agents mapped the full link graph, classified each broken URL, and produced a country-aware redirect report in **15 hours**. A traditional agency had previously scoped the same work at 6 months and 600+ engineering hours.

| Dimension | Traditional Agency Scope | Berlin |
|---|---|---|
| Pages audited | 2,500,000 | 2,500,000 |
| Broken URLs classified | 12,000 | 12,000 |
| Scoped delivery time | ~~6 months~~ | **15 hours** |
| Engineering hours | ~~600+~~ | **< 15** |
| Country-aware routing | ~~Manual~~ | **Automated** |

**What this means:**
Delivered as a pilot underneath an agency running Berlin against their own client work — substrate proof, and the result that taught us to **be** the agency rather than power one. The April 2026 repositioning came directly from this case. The substrate (Snake.blue, cached data, proprietary data routing) is the reason one Berlin FDM ships agency-scoped work in agency-fraction time — that's where the 1/4-of-agency pricing anchor comes from.

## Slide 9 — Business Model & Pricing

**Eyebrow:** BUSINESS MODEL
**Headline:** Priced at approximately one-quarter of the equivalent agency cost. Scope-dependent. Not a tool seat.
**Subhead:** ~80% gross margin on platform infrastructure. LLM compute pass-through at cost.

**Pricing principle:** Berlin's price for any given customer is anchored at ~**1/4 of what an agency would charge for the same scope of work**. Scope determines the absolute number; the agency-fraction anchor stays constant.

**Scope ladder:**

| Scope tier | Agency-equivalent retainer | Berlin price | Example buyer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Entry** | $1.5K–$2K/mo | **$499** | Local clinic, single-location service business (Reach Psych shape) |

**ARPU expansion** comes from mix shift across this ladder, not from price increases on existing accounts. The current launch cohort sits at the Entry tier deliberately — clean reference cohort, then walk subsequent cohorts up.

**Primary motion:** *Berlin + FDM* — full end-to-end SEO/AEO execution with an assigned Forward Deployed Marketer. One product, one motion.
