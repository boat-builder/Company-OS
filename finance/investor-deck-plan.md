**Eyebrow:** THE PROBLEM
**Headline:** SEO requires three pillars working together. Today the agency is the only credible way to cover all three — and the agency is broken.
**Subhead:** Strategy (what + why), execution (doing the work), tools (the stack to act). Companies hire an agency. Even in-house marketing teams hire an agency for the grunt work. Everyone ends up at the same broken contract.

**2x2 grid of pain cards:**

**4. AEO/GEO is not covered by either path.** Agencies are learning AI search on the customer's dime, and the in-house teams who hire them inherit the gap. The work is expanding faster than headcount can absorb.
