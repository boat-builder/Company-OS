## 1. Audience Snapshot

**Who they are:** Agency owners and SEO directors running 5–20 client accounts with lean teams (typically 3–12 people). They're operationally stretched — every new client adds reporting, research, and delivery burden that doesn't scale with their current tooling or headcount.

**Where they are in their journey:** Most have a working SEO practice but are hitting a ceiling. They're aware AI search is reshaping the landscape and feel pressure from peers and clients to have an answer. They've likely tried stitching together automations with n8n, Make, or raw API calls — and hit reliability walls.

**Buyer roles:**

- **Primary decision-maker:** Agency Owner / Managing Director
- **Champion / evaluator:** Director of SEO, Head of Operations
- **Day-to-day user:** Senior SEO Strategist, Account Manager

## 2. Positioning Statement

Berlin is the programmable infrastructure that lets a medium agency serve more clients at higher quality — without hiring proportionally. One platform replaces the fragmented toolstack, automates the manual work eating your team's capacity, and gives you a credible AI visibility offering to sell as a new service line.

**One-liner for landing pages / ads:**

> Serve more clients. Hire fewer people. Sell AI visibility.

---

## 3. The Problem Narrative

_Use this framing in decks, landing page hero sections, and top-of-funnel content._

Every new client your agency signs means more reports to build, more GSC accounts to monitor, more rank data to pull, more competitor analysis to run — and it all happens manually across 4–6 tools that don't talk to each other. Your team is spending 15–20 hours per client per week on manual research and reporting instead of strategy.

Meanwhile, clients are asking about AI search visibility — "are we showing up in ChatGPT?" — and you don't have a scalable answer. Competitors are already pitching AI SEO packages. The agencies who operationalize this first win the next wave of client mandates. The ones who can't will lose accounts to the ones who can.

You don't need more headcount. You need infrastructure that makes your existing team a force multiplier.

## 4. Messaging Pillars

### Pillar A: "Build once, run across every client"

The agentic workflow engine lets you build a reporting workflow, content audit, or competitor analysis once and run it across all client accounts. The workflow marketplace gives you pre-built, expert-level workflows that work out of the box — no building from scratch required. What used to take one person per five clients now runs automatically.

**Key features to reference:** Agentic Workflow Builder, Workflow Marketplace, Workflow Scheduling, Workflow Sharing

### Pillar B: "One subscription replaces the entire toolstack"

Berlin's integrated keyword intelligence, proprietary crawlers (Snake.blue), and unified data layer (GSC, GA4, Bing Webmaster, CMS, and more) mean you cancel Ahrefs, Semrush, your crawl tool, your rank tracker, and your reporting tool. One platform. One bill. No more data stitching.

**Key features to reference:** Keyword Intelligence, Site Crawling & Competitor Monitoring, Unified Data Access Layer, Third-Party Integrations

### Pillar C: "Sell AI visibility as a premium service"

GEO/AEO monitoring and optimization workflows let you offer clients a genuinely new service — AI search visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews. This isn't a feature you add to existing reports. It's a new revenue line.

**Key features to reference:** Ranking Signal Intelligence, Agentic Workflow Marketplace (GEO/AEO workflows)

### Pillar D: "No developer needed"

The workflow builder is chat-based. Describe what you need in plain English, and Berlin builds a structured, multi-step workflow. No code, no node graphs, no API auth headaches. And because Berlin routes data through its own infrastructure (not the LLM's context window), you get accurate results even on complex, multi-source operations — unlike standard AI automations that hallucinate as they scale.

**Key features to reference:** Agentic Workflow Builder, Thin MCP Architecture

### Pillar E: "Consistent quality at scale"

Brand context stores each client's guidelines, terminology, and preferences. Every workflow output — whoever on your team runs it — stays on-brand. The Review Center adds human-in-the-loop approval before anything goes to clients.

**Key features to reference:** Brand Context, Review Center, Team & Org Management

## 8. Pricing Framing

_How to talk about price with this ICP._

**Anchor to labor, not software.** A fully loaded SEO specialist costs $60K–$120K/year. Berlin at $199/month (Founding Partner pricing) or $800–$1,200/month (outcome-based) is a fraction of one hire — and it scales across all clients simultaneously.

**Current phase:** Founding Partner pricing at $199/month flat + credit top-ups. Position this as early-adopter advantage before pricing moves to $300–$500/month (Phase 2) and $800–$1,200/month (Phase 3).

**Consolidation math:** If they're spending $500–$2,000+/month across separate tools, Berlin can be cost-neutral or cost-negative even before counting the labor savings.

## 9. Tone & Voice for This ICP

- **Ambitious but pragmatic.** These are builders. They don't want hype — they want to see the system work.
- **Peer-to-peer, not vendor-to-buyer.** Talk like a fellow operator who understands agency economics.
- **Growth-forward.** The framing is about scaling up, not just surviving. "Punch above your weight class" resonates.
- **Show, don't tell.** Screenshots, workflow demos, and before/after comparisons carry more weight than feature lists.
- **Urgency without fear.** The competitive pressure is real — lean into FOMO on AI search, but from a "get ahead" angle, not a "you're falling behind" one.
