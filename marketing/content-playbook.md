# Marketing Content Guidelines

## Core Principles
- The core messaging and positioning must be intact throughout the content.
- Content is the GTM infrastructure.
- Convert sales call transcripts and ideas into narratives that fit the positioning and backstory.
- Content is not where you sell. It is where you earn the right to sell later.

## Audience Definition
Before publishing, answer clearly: who is this for?
- A defined role or buying persona.
- A defined company profile.
- A defined set of problems they recognize as urgent.

Know who you are speaking to at the narrowest level possible. It's ok to have multiple ICPs. Decide which industry, segment, or buyer you want to dominate and make that obvious in every post.

## Content Goals
Every piece of content should have a primary goal. Do not mix objectives within the same post.

**Brand Positioning**: Earn attention through strong opinions, sharp market commentary, and clear points of view. Make the right audience stop scrolling and recognize how you think.

**Pipeline Generation**: Reduce uncertainty. Demonstrate execution. Make your approach legible. Provide buyers with confidence. Use tactical breakdowns, system explanations, case studies, and milestone posts.

## Content Funnel
The funnel is not about calls to action. It is about specificity.

- **TOFU**: Speak broadly to a role or industry. Identify shared pain, market shifts, or emerging patterns. Attract the right audience and filter out the wrong one.
- **MOFU**: Speak directly to your ICP's lived experience. Demonstrate operational-level understanding. This is where trust builds quietly.
- **BOFU**: Be highly specific. Reduce risk. Show momentum. Make your execution visible.

## Content Requirements
Every post should do at least one of three things:
- Educate.
- Relate.
- Signal momentum.

If it does none of those, do not publish it.

## Execution
Strong content gets three elements right:
- The hook earns attention.
- The body delivers value.
- The structure reinforces clarity.

Most effort should go into the hook and the clarity of the argument. If the hook fails, the body will never be read. Once attention is earned, the body's job is to justify that attention.

## Outbound from Content
Every interaction with content is an opportunity for outbound because the lead is warm.

When you outbound:
- Reference the specific trigger for outreach.
- Provide something useful or relevant.
- Avoid leading with a meeting request.

Lead with insight. Offer a perspective that builds on what they engaged with. Provide value that would be worthwhile even if no call is booked.

For every piece of content, have a "conversation carrier" built and ready that eventually opens up the sales conversation. Personalization is not optional. Every message should feel intentional.
