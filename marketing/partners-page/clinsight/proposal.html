One clinic per market

The local map has only ~3 top slots — so no one can put two competing clinics first at the same time. Our policy is one clinic per city + specialty.

It's a feature, not a limit: it protects your clients from the rival down the street buying the same edge from us. If you already have two competing clinics, we differentiate them by sub-specialty, service line, or neighborhood so they win different searches.

Compliance & approvals

Healthcare content needs sign-off before it goes live. The clinic's designated licensed reviewer approves and owns compliance — we draft, they verify.

To keep delivery moving, we recommend a 2–3 business-day approval window. For markets like India with lighter requirements, this can be streamlined.
