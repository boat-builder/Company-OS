Under the hood

Why Berlin doesn't hallucinate

You've seen AI tools produce garbage on SEO data. Here's why Berlin is different.

Your Team
Describes the task in plain English — "Generate weekly ranking report for Client X across 6 countries"
▼
LLM Layer
Orchestrates & reasons — plans the steps, decides what data to pull, structures the output. Never touches raw data directly.
▼
Berlin MCP
Handles all data retrieval & processing — keywords, crawls, GSC, GA4, competitor data. Large datasets stay out of the LLM context window. Accurate results on 2.5M URL sites.
▼
Data Sources
GSC · GA4 · Client CMS · Keyword DB · Proprietary crawlers · Competitor monitors — all unified, all queryable, per client

Standard AI tools push everything through the LLM's context window. When the data is large or semantically similar — which is constant in SEO — the output is unreliable. Berlin's architecture solves this at the infrastructure level.
