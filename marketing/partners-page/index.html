Why this deck exists

SEO in 2026 is roughly 10× harder than it was three years ago.

The blue-link game became the AI-search game — and the playbook for the new game isn’t written down anywhere.

Surface expanded

You’re no longer optimizing for one search engine. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google’s AI Mode, traditional Google, AI overviews — same buyer, six surfaces.

Scope expanded

Review velocity, citation building, brand presence on the platforms LLMs cite from (Reddit, LinkedIn, niche forums), authority signals across multiple platforms — none of it on a 2022 SEO checklist.

No playbook

Google and OpenAI publish nothing. The techniques that work shift week to week. Agencies and in-house teams don’t have the bandwidth to run the experiments.

BERLIN The old way03 / 13
The agency model

Agencies were built for the world before AI Search.

  • Quarterly audits.A real audit cycle is three days of human work. Run once a quarter, sometimes once.
  • Narrow surface area.Built for blue links. AI Search citations, review velocity, and platform authority sit outside the standard scope.
  • Slow turnarounds.Brief, draft, review, revise, push to CMS — weeks per page, billed by the hour.
  • Priced for human throughput.$4K–$15K/mo for mid-market scope. The math forces narrow coverage.

When you sign an agency today, you’re paying for yesterday’s playbook — delivered with tomorrow’s results delayed.

Among customers already paying an agency, dissatisfaction is near-universal in our discovery calls. Not because agency teams aren’t good — the model itself doesn’t fit 2026.

The substrate

This isn’t a wrapper. There’s real infrastructure underneath.

Three pieces of engineering that the comparison numbers actually rest on. You won’t see any of this in the product — you’ll just notice that the answers are current and the workflows don’t break.

Our own crawler
Snake.blue.

Berlin runs its own AI-first crawler against your site and your competitors continuously. What you see in the audit is current — not a Semrush export from last month.

Our own data layer
Queryable, not guessed.

Millions of keywords and pages sit behind a fast query layer that agents hit directly. Not retrieved from an LLM’s context window, not hallucinated. The reason long, multi-step audit runs don’t fall over.

80+ ranking signals
Tracked automatically.

The signal capture and prioritization that, at an agency, would be an analyst’s full week of manual work. At Berlin, it’s a tool the platform calls in the background.

Why we bother saying this: it’s the reason one Dedicated Marketer can cover the depth an agency assigns three people to — and why pricing lands at ~1/4 of agency cost without breaking the unit economics.
