## 01 · The coverage dial

The capability is identical everywhere. The only thing the dial moves is *who executes* — and with it, the margin profile.

`You run it ──────────────── We run it`
`high margin · product signal      high load · service signal`

| Position | Mode | Who executes | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Guided** *(NOT SOLD YET — future product path)* | Self-run | We stand up & maintain the system and hand you the prioritized roadmap + exact tasks. **Your team executes.** | Highest margin · validates the **product** path |
| **Co-Pilot** | Shared | We run the agentic execution; your team handles judgment & brand pieces and approvals. **Split delivery.** | The blended middle |
| **Fully-Run** | Managed | We execute end-to-end. Your team is approval-only or fully offhand. **We run all of it.** | Highest human load · validates **service** demand |

## 02 · What sits inside every tier

Tier by **coverage, never by capability.** The intelligence layer — the part traditional SEO teams structurally can't offer — is present in all three tiers and leads the pitch. Only the execution layer changes hands.

**Always-on intelligence layer** — *identical in every tier · the wedge · mostly Bucket A (scalable)*
- LLM source intelligence — which sources the models actually pull your category's answers from, mapped to your real prompts & keywords
- AEO experiment engine, tuned by segment
- Competitive intelligence — what rivals do and where they win
- Observers & monitors running continuously
- The prioritized roadmap that falls out of it (the year's top moves)
- System stood up *and kept current* as models & tooling shift

**Execution layer** — *what the dial moves · who runs it changes · Bucket B (human) concentrates here*
- On-page — new content, refreshes, optimization
- Technical — including AEO schema & structured-data work
- Off-site / source-targeted presence — Reddit, LinkedIn, UGC, reviews, wherever the LLMs source

> **The cannibalization line.** Every human task in the execution layer is product backlog. Drive Fully-Run toward Guided's margin profile over time. The day we stop cannibalizing, we're an agency again.
>
> **Pre-product corollary (2026-06): Co-Pilot is the profitable wedge; Fully-Run is the margin trap *and* the growth tier.** Until the product carries the execution layer, Fully-Run makes *us* supply the labor at our cost, while Co-Pilot lets the *customer's team* supply it at theirs. So land on Co-Pilot for margin today — but Fully-Run is where the human-task backlog (the thing the product is built to absorb) and the bigger ACV come from. Lead with the coverage that fits the buyer; don't religiously push either.

## 04 · How to price a cell

```
Platform base  +  Coverage load  +  Scale sizing
```

- **Platform base** is constant — the always-on intelligence layer, your high-margin floor.
- **Coverage load** rises left → right as execution shifts to you.
- **Scale sizing** rises top → bottom with surface area.

**Activation is absorbed** into the first weeks of coverage — no separate setup line, no discount gimmick. The front-loaded ~80–90% just draws from early coverage; the thin perpetual tail draws from it forever, sold as *"we keep your system current."*

**Current working floors (2026-06-22 · small surface · revisit as cost/PMF clarifies):**
- `CP·S` (Co-Pilot, "your team owns execution") ≈ **$2,000 /mo** — cost-discipline target **< $1k** to hold ~60% contribution.
- `FR·S` (Fully-Run, "we run everything") ≈ **$4,000 /mo** — cost-discipline target **~$1.5k** to hold ~60% contribution.
- Lesson banked: the old "$500 margin" worry was a Fully-Run delivery cost (~$1.5k) charged at a Co-Pilot price ($2k). Price the *mode* to the *cost*. The $4k agency a real prospect turned down confirms the market tolerates this range — we sit beneath it as the value option.
- We under-priced our first Co-Pilot customer at $799; **price every new deal at the floor**, not the comfort number.

**Beyond-envelope work** (a new product line, market, language, or source ecosystem) is an **expansion conversation** with an existing customer — never raised at the cold close.

## 05 · The AEO-led consult front-door (exploratory · 2026-06-24)

> **Exploration, not a live SKU.** A separate motion from the 9-cell managed grid above — a deliberate experimental track for entering **10–50M ARR** companies where the cold $2k offer doesn't reach and we have no testimonials. See evolution log steps 17–22 and the ICP exploratory-expansion section. The 1–10M founder-led grid motion is unchanged and remains the baseline.

**What it is:** a **high-ticket, fixed-scope engagement** — "stand up your AI-native AEO/SEO system" — sold on the **founder's expertise and thesis**, not on case studies. It's the testimonial-free key into a bigger company. Lead with **AEO** (no incumbent, no references exist for anyone), expand into SEO/organic once inside.

**Why it works where the cold offer can't:** consulting is bought on conviction and track record, so it sidesteps the logo-wall the upmarket buyer normally demands. It also revives the **parked Guided tier** — Guided-shaped work *is* sellable today because the founder is in the room stabilizing what the product can't yet self-run.

**The three guardrails (or this becomes an agency):**
1. **Substrate = our product**, somewhere we control — *not* "install into your stack." Be willing to lose the deal that demands otherwise; without the substrate there's no flywheel.
2. **Productize + price to convert.** Fixed scope, fixed deliverable, explicitly designed to *end on the managed dial* — the exit of the consult is the entrance to the managed relationship. Not open-ended advisory that renews as advisory.
3. **Feed reusable backlog.** Every install must populate the execution ledger and make the next one cheaper, or it's just a job the founder bought himself.

**Pricing posture (number not set):** high-ticket relative to the $2k–$4k/mo managed floors, sold as a one-time scoped fee with a **planned conversion to a managed retainer** afterward. Set the absolute number against delivery cost + the value of door-opening into a 10–50M account; the consult fee can run leaner than pure-consulting rates *because* the real return is the managed expansion it lands. **Build nothing infrastructural to sell it** — no new website/brand; reach out as an individual until the bet proves out.

**Run it as a bet:** consult aimed at **10–50M only** (keep the 1–10M cold offer clean so signals stay readable), with a pre-committed signal + kill date. SEO-side upmarket expansion stays **trigger-gated** (2 referenceable accounts); AEO-side entry is *now*.

## Operating rules

1. **No prices on the website.** The price is reached on a call — pre-PMF, that call is the single richest data source, and a page price throws it away.
2. **One decision, one scoping step.** Customer picks coverage; you scope scale. The grid is yours and stays behind the curtain.
3. **Sell coverage, cost it in hours.** Never bill hours — efficiency gains become more output at the same price, not a smaller invoice.
4. **Hunt narrow, serve wide.** Outbound only to your 2–3 sharp segment bets (≥1 vertical); take any inbound off-ICP customer for the revenue and the data.
