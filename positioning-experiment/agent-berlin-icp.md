# Agent Berlin — ICP (who we sell to)

> **Internal.** The *person/team profile* we hunt — not the vertical (still open). Read alongside `agent-berlin-price-grid.md` and `agent-berlin-evolution-log.md` (steps 12–16). Reflects the saleable surface as of **2026-06-22**: only **Co-Pilot** and **Fully-Run** are live; **Guided / product-only is parked** until the product can self-run.

## The bullseye (hunt this)

**The founder — or founder-delegated growth/marketing lead — of a fast-growing B2B / B2B2C SaaS or marketplace (~$20k–$150k+ MRR and climbing) that already has a 1–3 person in-house marketing + SEO function.**

This is not a guess; it's the customer we already have (the job platform, step 14). The job now is to find twenty more of exactly this.

### Why this one profile clears every constraint at once

- **Authority + speed.** Founder signs — a fast, single-threaded yes. No procurement, no committee.
- **$2k+ is easy, not a stretch.** At their scale $2k/mo is a rounding error and roughly *half* the $4k agency they'd otherwise consider. The price respects them without scaring them.
- **It's a reallocation, not new money.** They already spend on SEO / content / an agency line — we redirect existing budget instead of fighting for a new one (the fast pre-PMF path).
- **Their team makes us profitable.** A 1–3 person marketing+SEO team can carry execution → **Co-Pilot** → our delivery cost stays low → ~60% contribution. (Pre-product, this is the whole margin story — see step 14.)
- **Reachable without testimonials.** Tech founders buy on *thesis and conviction* and live on LinkedIn — exactly where founder-led, build-in-public content reaches them. Our lack of a logo wall doesn't sink the sale; the **one honest proof we have** carries it: *they evaluated a $4k agency they liked and chose us instead.* **(Correction, 2026-06-25: we do NOT claim "we grew them 20k→100k" — that growth predated us; they became a client 2 weeks ago already at that scale. The customer is a referral source + a pitch-credibility signal, not a results case study. See evolution log step 34.)**
- **Built-in expansion.** They're growing fast → more surface, more coverage over time → the account climbs from a $2k Co-Pilot start toward Fully-Run and larger scale. Land-and-expand is native here, which is what turns a low-ACV grind into a real business.

## How we sell to them (ties to the price grid)

- **One external offer, not two buckets.** The customer never picks "Co-Pilot" or "Fully-Run." They have **one offer**, and we scope it via an internal **coverage dial** ("how hands-on do you want to be — run it with your team, or have us run it all?") and an internal **scale dial** (surface). "Co-Pilot / Fully-Run" are *vocabulary for the conversation*, never products on a price card. (Why: two SKUs force premature self-classification, anchor a "why double?" comparison, and throw away our biggest margin lever — how much their team owns. See evolution log step 16.)
- **One number on the call.** The 9-cell grid gives us discrete internal anchors; the customer experiences a single scoped price. No customer-facing slider.
- **Working floors:** ~**$2k/mo** at the "your team owns execution" end, ~**$4k/mo** at the "we run everything" end, scaled by surface. Price the *mode* to the *cost*; price every new deal at the floor.

## Exploratory expansion — the 10–50M segment via an AEO-led consult (2026-06-24)

> **Exploration, not a committed ICP shift.** The bullseye above (1–10M-ish, founder-led) stands and stays primary. This is a *second, deliberately experimental track* aimed higher. See evolution log steps 17–22.

**Target:** an **established 10–50M ARR B2B company** — the buyer the table below marks "Avoid now" (Head of Growth / VP / CMO, real budget, multi-threaded, normally reference-hungry).

**Why we can now consider the buyer we previously parked — two things changed the math:**

- **AEO neutralizes the testimonial gap.** Nobody has AEO case studies yet — the whole market is at zero — so the logo-wall expectation that makes upmarket SEO a reference-hungry knife fight largely evaporates *in AEO*. We sell on thesis and on being visibly ahead. (Lead with AEO; the SEO/organic work is the *expansion* sold once inside and trusted.)
- **A consult front-door sells on the founder's expertise, not case studies.** High-ticket consulting is bought on conviction and track record, so it gets us in the door of a bigger company *without* the references we lack — then we install the product as the substrate and let trust convert cold-unsellable managed work into warm expansion.

**The strength, in buyer language:** *"your agency literally cannot see what we see"* — we map which sources the LLMs actually pull the category's answers from and act on it at a speed/cost an SEO-era agency or in-house team can't. In AEO their SEO experience is partly a liability, not a moat.

**Guardrails (so this stays a product company, not an agency):** product is the substrate (not their stack — lose the deal that demands otherwise); the consult is productized and priced to convert to the managed dial; every engagement feeds reusable backlog. See price grid §05.

**How it runs:** as a sharp bet *behind* the 1–10M baseline — outreach as an individual, no new website/brand, consult aimed at 10–50M only (keep the 1–10M cold offer clean so signals stay readable), with a pre-committed signal + kill date. SEO-side upmarket expansion is **trigger-gated** (2 referenceable accounts), AEO-side entry is *now*.

## What's still open

- **Vertical.** This persona clusters naturally in **B2B SaaS / marketplaces**, which is also where AI-search anxiety runs hottest — so persona and vertical may collapse into one bet. Not yet committed; decide separately.
- **Repeatability.** The profile is drawn from one paying customer of this shape. Confirm it (and the Co-Pilot-is-the-wedge hypothesis) on the next 3–4 deals before treating it as settled.
- **Which engine wins.** Whether the proven 1–10M founder-led motion or the exploratory 10–50M consult front-door becomes the primary growth engine is left to *observation* (~2 months, on close-rate / margin / energy), not pre-chosen.
