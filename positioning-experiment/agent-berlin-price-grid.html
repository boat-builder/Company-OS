02 What sits inside every tier

Tier by coverage, never by capability. The intelligence layer — the part traditional SEO teams structurally can't offer — is present in all three tiers and leads the pitch. Only the execution layer changes hands.

Always-on intelligence layer

Identical in every tier · the wedge · mostly Bucket A
  • LLM source intelligence — which sources the models actually pull your category's answers from, mapped to your real prompts & keywords
  • AEO experiment engine, tuned by segment
  • Competitive intelligence — what rivals do and where they win
  • Observers & monitors running continuously
  • The prioritized roadmap that falls out of it (the year's top moves)
  • System stood up and kept current as models & tooling shift

Execution layer

What the dial moves · who runs it changes · Bucket B concentrates here
  • On-page — new content, refreshes, optimization
  • Technical — including AEO schema & structured-data work
  • Off-site / source-targeted presence — Reddit, LinkedIn, UGC, reviews, wherever the LLMs source

The cannibalization line. Every human task here is product backlog. Drive Fully-Run toward Guided's margin profile over time.

04 How to price a cell · operating rules

Cell composition

Platform base + Coverage load + Scale sizing

Platform base is constant — the always-on intelligence layer, your high-margin floor. Coverage load rises left → right as execution shifts to you. Scale sizing rises top → bottom with surface area.

Activation is absorbed into the first weeks of coverage — no separate setup line, no discount gimmick. The front-loaded ~80–90% just draws from early coverage; the thin perpetual tail draws from it forever, sold as "we keep your system current."

Beyond-envelope work (a new product line, market, language, or source ecosystem) is an expansion conversation with an existing customer — never raised at the cold close.

Operating rules

  1. No prices on the website. The price is reached on a call — pre-PMF, that call is the single richest data source, and a page price throws it away.
  2. One decision, one scoping step. Customer picks coverage; you scope scale. The grid is yours and stays behind the curtain.
  3. Sell coverage, cost it in hours. Never bill hours — efficiency gains become more output at the same price, not a smaller invoice.
  4. Hunt narrow, serve wide. Outbound only to your 2–3 sharp segment bets (≥1 vertical); take any inbound off-ICP customer for the revenue and the data.
