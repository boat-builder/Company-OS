## Messaging — What Works / What Doesn't

### 2026-04-23 Repositioning Note
On 2026-04-23 Berlin repositioned from programmable workflow infrastructure (where users build their own agentic SEO workflows, humans bring the strategy) to an end-to-end SEO/AEO platform organized around three pillars: Strategy (what to do), Execution (doing it), and Tools (Semrush, GSC/GA4, CMS, browser, etc.). Berlin now owns all three pillars by default, with users optionally bringing their own strategy. The alternative we replace is hiring an SEO agency or in-house team — not workflow tools. USOL is internal infrastructure, not a product. ICP is now the 6-cell grid in [customers/icp.md](customers/icp.md) (SaaS / eCommerce / Lead Gen × Founder or Marketing Leader), not the prior agency/Growth-Eng/SMB mix. Messaging learnings below were validated under the old positioning and many may not generalize — prune or re-learn as new signal arrives.

### Lead with the SEO data layer, not connectors
Connectors (GSC, GA4, Bing) feel like table stakes to power users — especially those already on Make or AirOps. The real differentiator is two things working together: **(1) the SEO data layer is native and pre-loaded** — no per-client OAuth wiring, no setup; and **(2) the workflow builder starts from SEO context**, not general automation you have to teach SEO to. That's a product category difference, not a feature comparison.

_2026-04-23 flag:_ Learned under old positioning (programmable workflow infra targeting agency power users on Make/AirOps). Likely does not generalize — under the new end-to-end positioning, the comparison is agencies/in-house hires, not workflow automation tools, and the "data layer + workflow builder" framing is no longer the surface we sell. Suspect; re-validate or retire.

### ICP-1 messaging anchor
**"Build once, all clients, no setup."** Lead with the operational multiplier. Avoid feature lists.

_2026-04-23 flag:_ Learned under old positioning for the retired agency ICP (ICP-1 was Medium Agencies). The "build once, all clients" multiplier is an agency-operator value prop and does not map to the new 6-cell grid of direct SaaS / eCom / Lead-Gen buyers. Does not apply under new positioning; keep as historical record only.

### "Early stage" framing — when to use it
Avoid in cold outreach to Director-level and above. It signals risk to people accountable for client workflows. Reserve for warm follow-up once genuine curiosity is established.

_2026-04-23 flag:_ Partially generalizes. The underlying insight — "early stage" reads as risk to senior buyers accountable for outcomes — is buyer-psychology, not positioning-specific, and likely still holds for the Marketing Leader / CMO cells. The specific "client workflows" framing is old-positioning (agency-shaped); the principle survives even though the wording needs updating.
