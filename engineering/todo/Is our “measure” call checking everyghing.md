**What Measure asks Search Console, one line each:**

1. **Page totals** (once per press): every page of the site that showed in Google search over the range, with clicks, impressions and average position. It gives each priority page its figures, and Google’s spelling of its address.
2. **Index check** (once per priority page): whether Google has the page indexed, which canonical Google picked versus the site’s, when Google last crawled it, and whether robots.txt or fetching blocked it.
3. **Page searches** (once per priority page that showed in search): the searches that showed that page, with clicks, impressions and position for each.
4. **Branded searches** (once per brand term, which for miniOrange is just “miniorange”): every search containing the brand name, added up. **\[Question: should we handle a few variants like mini orange, miniorange etc]**
5. **Keyword pages** (once per question keyword): which of the site’s pages Google showed for that exact keyword, with their figures.
