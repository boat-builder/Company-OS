## Key Pain Points

- **They are doing the job of three people.** Technical SEO, content strategy, reporting, link building, competitive research — one or two people are responsible for all of it, and the surface area keeps expanding.
- **They never get developer time.** Any task that requires engineering involvement simply doesn't get done.
- **Their tooling budget is tight.** Paying for multiple tool subscriptions is hard to justify when headcount is also squeezed.
- **AI search is a major stressor.** They know things are changing but have no time to learn a new framework, let alone implement one.
- **Reporting to leadership is painful.** They can't confidently explain AI's impact on SEO with data to back it up.
- **They feel behind.** Larger companies have teams, developers, and tools. The gap is growing.

---

## Why Deprioritized

- Budget constraints limit willingness-to-pay at Berlin's target price points ($199/month launch, $800–$1,200/month outcome-based).
- Limited scale benefits — Berlin's "build once, run across many" value prop doesn't resonate with a single-site operator.
- Founders doing their own SEO are not the primary buyers for infrastructure — they outsource or hire.
- This segment may be better served by the free workflow library (PLG tier) once it launches, without dedicated GTM effort.

---

## Possible Re-Entry Conditions

- If Berlin launches a lightweight free/low-cost tier with pre-built workflows that serves this segment at near-zero marginal cost.
- If Berlin launches a services wing (under active consideration) that continues to serve individual operators.
- If usage data from the free tier shows unexpectedly high conversion rates from SMB/startup users.

---

## Ideal Roles

- **SEO Manager** — Often the only SEO person; buyer, user, and champion in one.
- **Growth Marketing Manager** — SEO is part of a broader mandate; values tools that consolidate workflows.
- **Content Marketing Manager** — May own SEO by default; needs accessible automation.
- **Head of Marketing (at early-stage startup)** — Wears many hats including SEO; time-starved.
- **Founder / Co-Founder** — At very early stages, may be doing SEO themselves; extremely time-constrained.
