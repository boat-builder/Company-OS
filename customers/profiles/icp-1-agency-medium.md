## Key Pain Points

- **Reporting has exploded in volume and complexity.** Clients are panicking about CTR drops and SERP volatility. Every account now demands more explanation, more data, more reassurance — and there's no scalable way to produce that manually across 5–20 clients.
- **They're being asked about AI search and don't have good answers.** GEO/AEO is new territory. Clients are asking "are we visible in ChatGPT?" and most agencies are either winging it or ignoring the question. They need a structured, repeatable way to address it.
- **No developer access to build automations.** They know what they want to automate (rank checks, content briefs, internal link audits, client reports) but they can't build it. They're stuck in spreadsheets and manual exports. Even the more advanced ones who try tools like n8n or Make hit a wall — setting up API authentication is technically heavy, and handling data with a lot of MCPs increases hallucination and leads to silly, unnoticed mistakes.
- **Managing data across 5–20 client accounts is a coordination nightmare.** GSC, GA4, rank data, and crawl data all live in different places. There's no single query layer. Every report is a bespoke manual job.
- **They're paying for 4–6 separate tools that don't talk to each other.** Ahrefs + Semrush + crawl tool + reporting + rank tracker + maybe a GEO monitoring tool. The cost adds up ($500–$2,000+/month in aggregate), and the data is still siloed.
- **Team consistency breaks at scale.** Different team members produce outputs that look and sound different. Without a shared source of truth (brand context per client), quality control is an ongoing problem.
- **Competitive pressure is mounting.** Other agencies are pitching "AI SEO" capabilities. The agencies who can't operationalize it will lose clients to ones who can.
- **SEO/AEO is evolving at high speed.** New things to do, new reports to make, new things to monitor appear frequently — increasing workload until they either ignore these new requirements or do a bad job handling them.

## Core Value Proposition

Berlin is the force multiplier that lets a medium agency punch above its weight class — serving more clients at higher quality without proportional headcount growth.

**Build once, run across all clients.** The agentic workflow builder replaces the manual, per-client work that's eating their team's time. Build a reporting workflow, a content audit, a competitor analysis once — then run it across every client account. The agentic workflow marketplace gives them immediate access to pre-built, vetted workflows that handle complex multi-step operations with a single click — no need to build from scratch. Teams get automation value on day one.

**One subscription replaces many.** Berlin's integrated keyword intelligence (sourced from Semrush/DataForSEO, cached in Berlin's own DB), proprietary crawlers (Snake.blue), and expanding integrations (GSC, GA4, Bing Webmaster Tools, CMS, social) mean agencies don't need separate subscriptions for keyword data, crawling, rank tracking, and workflow automation. One platform covers data, integrations, and orchestration.

**Sell AI visibility as a new service line.** The GEO/AEO capabilities give them a genuine differentiator — they can show clients AI visibility data across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and deliver structured recommendations, not just organic rankings.

**Works where they already work.** Berlin's thin MCP interoperability layer exposes the full platform inside agentic coding environments — Claude Code, Claude Cowork, ChatGPT Codex, Openclaw — with accuracy guarantees that standard MCP implementations can't provide. Berlin's architecture routes data through its own unified layer rather than filling the LLM's context window, solving the hallucination problem that breaks complex workflows in other tools.

**Brand context keeps outputs consistent.** Organizational knowledge persists across every interaction and workflow — per client, per project. Outputs stay on-brand regardless of who on the team runs them.

**Forward Deployed Marketers available.** For agencies that want accelerated time-to-value, Berlin's own marketing specialists can embed with the agency to design, build, and optimize agentic workflows. Priced at $2,000+/month, this also produces battle-tested workflow templates that feed back into the platform.

**In short:** more clients, same team size, better deliverables, a new service line to sell, and one subscription that replaces a fragmented toolstack.

## Assumptions About Their Priorities

- **Retaining existing clients is more urgent than acquiring new ones.** Churn risk is high when clients feel their agency doesn't understand AI search.
- **They are willing to pay for tools that reduce headcount pressure.** Cost is a secondary concern if the tool replaces 1–3 additional hires worth of work. They compare Berlin to a headcount ($60K–$120K/year), not a tool ($200/month per seat).
- **FOMO is a real purchasing driver.** They've seen peers positioning around GEO/AEO and feel behind. Speed to a credible offering matters.
- **Multi-client management must be native.** A tool that works per-account but doesn't scale across accounts will not stick.
- **They want to look sophisticated to clients**, not just work faster internally. Client-facing outputs (reports, audits, AI visibility dashboards) are as important as internal efficiency.
- **Tool consolidation is a meaningful value driver.** Reducing from 4–6 separate subscriptions to one platform saves money and eliminates data-stitching overhead.
- **Pre-built workflows dramatically lower adoption barriers.** They don't have time to build everything from scratch — the workflow marketplace lets them get value immediately and customize later.

## Ideal Roles

- **Agency Owner / Managing Director** — Decision-maker on tooling and positioning; feels competitive pressure directly. Cares about margin, growth, and differentiation.
- **Director of SEO / Head of SEO** — Owns delivery quality and team efficiency; will champion tools that reduce reporting burden and enable new service lines.
- **Head of Operations / COO** — Cares about margin, utilization, and scaling without proportional headcount growth.
- **Account Director / Client Success Lead** — Responsible for retention; motivated by anything that improves client-facing deliverables.
- **Senior SEO Strategist** — Hands-on user who will evaluate whether workflows actually save time.
