## Key Pain Points

- **Reporting at 20+ clients doesn't scale with headcount.** Every client demands customized reporting, increasing frequency, and more data. The agency is hiring just to keep up with deliverables, not to grow. Agencies report 15–20 hours per week per client on manual SEO research and strategy.
- **They're being asked about AI search and don't have a credible, scalable answer.** GEO/AEO questions from clients are growing. At this scale, they can't wing it client-by-client — they need a structured, repeatable offering. Client churn risk is high when they can't articulate an AI search story.
- **No developer access despite needing sophisticated automations.** At 20+ clients, the automation needs are complex: cross-client reporting, competitive intelligence at scale, automated content audits. But engineering resources are allocated to the agency's own products or client projects.
- **Data management across 20+ client accounts is a full-time job.** GSC, GA4, rank data, crawl data — multiplied by 20+ clients, each with unique configurations. No single query layer exists.
- **Team governance is essential but most tools don't support it.** Different team members working on different accounts need consistent processes, shared workflows, and human-in-the-loop review before client-facing outputs go out.
- **They're paying a fortune for fragmented tools.** At agency scale, tool costs compound — multiple Ahrefs/Semrush seats, crawl tools, reporting tools, rank trackers. The aggregate spend is $2,000+/month and the data is still siloed.
- **Capacity constraints are forcing them to turn away work or sacrifice quality.** They can't grow the client roster without proportional headcount — unless they automate.
- **The advanced ones trying automation with n8n/Make still struggle.** Setting up APIs with authentication is technically heavy, handling data with multiple MCPs increases LLM hallucination, and automations frequently break without anyone noticing. They end up falling back to manual work.

## Core Value Proposition

Everything the medium agency (ICP 1) gets, plus the governance and scale features that large agencies require.

**Build once, run across all clients at scale.** The agentic workflow builder and the workflow marketplace provide both custom and pre-built automation that works across 20+ client accounts. Build a reporting workflow once, run it across every client. The marketplace gives immediate access to vetted, complex workflows — no need to start from scratch.

**One subscription replaces the entire toolstack.** At 20+ clients, tool consolidation saves thousands per month. Berlin's integrated keyword intelligence, proprietary crawlers, and expanding integrations eliminate the need for separate Ahrefs, Semrush, crawl, and rank tracking subscriptions.

**Team governance and review.** Human-in-the-loop review center means outputs can be verified before going to clients. Team management with access controls, shared workflows, and org-level brand context ensures consistency at scale.

**Sell AI visibility as a premium service line.** GEO/AEO capabilities become a revenue center, not just a feature — agencies can build an entirely new service offering around AI search visibility.

**Thin MCP interoperability.** Berlin works inside the agentic coding environments teams already use (Claude Code, Claude Cowork, ChatGPT Codex, Openclaw) with accuracy guarantees that standard MCP implementations can't provide — solving the hallucination problem that breaks complex, multi-source workflows.

**Forward Deployed Marketers for high-touch onboarding.** Berlin's team embeds with the agency to design and optimize workflows, accelerating time-to-value and producing battle-tested templates. Priced at $2,000+/month, bundled into agency/enterprise contracts.

**In short:** scale delivery without proportional headcount, govern quality across the team, and add a premium AI visibility service line.

## Assumptions About Their Priorities

- **Capacity and margin are the top concerns.** At 20+ clients, every hour of manual work per client directly impacts profitability. Automation isn't a nice-to-have — it's how they maintain margins.
- **Governance and consistency are non-negotiable.** They've been burned by inconsistent outputs. Any tool must support team-level controls, review workflows, and shared brand context.
- **They want enterprise-grade reliability.** Uptime, data accuracy, and API stability matter more than feature breadth. They've experienced broken automations and won't tolerate it.
- **They compare the platform to headcount, not tools.** At $60K–$120K/year per hire, Berlin at $800–$1,200/month (outcome-based pricing) is trivially justified if it replaces one person's output.
- **Multi-client management is the evaluation criterion.** If it doesn't scale across 20+ accounts natively, it's disqualified.

## Ideal Roles

- **Agency Owner / Managing Director** — Decision-maker; feels margin pressure and competitive threat directly.
- **Director of SEO / Head of SEO** — Owns delivery quality; will champion tools that standardize and automate delivery.
- **Head of Operations / COO** — Owns utilization metrics and capacity planning; strongest internal advocate for automation.
- **Account Director / Client Success Lead** — Responsible for retention across the portfolio; motivated by client-facing quality improvements.
- **Senior SEO Strategist / Team Lead** — Hands-on evaluator; needs to trust the workflows before recommending adoption.
