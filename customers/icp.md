## Segment Hierarchy

### Primary ICP

The primary ICP is where product-market fit signals are strongest and where all GTM energy is focused during the Founding Partner phase.

| ICP | Segment | Core Pain | Value Hook | Top Roles | Buying Signal |
|-----|---------|-----------|------------|-----------|---------------|
| [**1**](profiles/icp-1-agency-medium.md) | Medium SEO / Marketing Agencies (5–20 clients) | Reporting doesn't scale; no GEO/AEO story; no dev access; need to grow without proportional headcount | Build once, run across all clients; sell AI visibility as new service; force multiplier to punch above weight class | Agency Owner, Director of SEO, Head of Ops | FOMO on AI SEO; client churn risk; growth-focused |

### Secondary ICPs

Secondary ICPs represent strong opportunities that Berlin can serve well but are not the focus of the initial GTM motion. They will be activated as the platform matures and the primary beachhead is established.

| ICP | Segment | Core Pain | Value Hook | Top Roles | Buying Signal |

| [**2**](profiles/icp-2-agency-large.md) | Large Agencies (20+ clients) | Reporting doesn't scale at volume; no GEO/AEO story; enterprise governance needs | Build once, run across all clients; sell AI visibility as new service; team governance & review | Agency Owner, Director of SEO, Head of Ops | FOMO on AI SEO; client churn risk; capacity constraints |

## Pricing Context (for ICP positioning)

Berlin's pricing is anchored to **labor replacement value**, not software benchmarks. A fully loaded SEO specialist costs $90–97K/year in the US. Agencies spend 50–60% of revenue on payroll. Customers compare Berlin to a headcount, not a tool — which supports $800–$1,200/month outcome-based pricing (Phase 3) vs. the $199/month launch price (Phase 1).

| Phase | Timeline | Pricing | Purpose |
|-------|----------|---------|---------|
| Phase 1 — Founding Partners | Now → April 2026 | $199/month flat + credit top-ups | Learning velocity; convert design partners |
| Phase 2 — Early Growth | May → August 2026 | Blended ARPU $300–$500/month | Expand customer base; tier segmentation begins |
| Phase 3 — Outcome-Based | September 2026+ | $800–$1,200/month | Anchored to labor replacement; case study-backed |
| Forward Deployed Marketers | Ongoing | $2,000+/month | Services layer; Berlin's team embeds with customer |
