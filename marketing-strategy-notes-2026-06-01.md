## The big decision: everything runs through the FDM motion

The unifying thread that emerged: **we are not selling the product standalone yet.** Every marketing piece, every ICP, every outreach is built around the FDM (Forward Deployed Marketer) model, where the client's internal team stays involved rather than fully offloading to an agency. The product or the process is capable of handling the requirement for just the product alone or full service like agency but thats not our business positioning for next few weeks.

Implication: the product needs features that let the internal team actually work inside it. We do have it but it needs to be evolved a bit more.

"Be the FDM guy." The goal is for the market to associate Sherin personally with FDM.

***

## ICPs

ICP is the common problem across **both** inbound and outbound. Needs to be listed explicitly so every activity is attributable to one ICP and trackable.

**Dropping:**

* **Local businesses** — Ananya will do one final test send (local SEO folks already researched) via Instantly to check if anything works. After that, we stop targeting this ICP.

**Focusing on:**

1. **Tech-savvy founders with reasonable revenue** — modeled on scale.jobs / Balaji. They want an FDM setup (their team stays involved), not a hands-off agency. Possibly narrow further to Indian founders or other shared traits.

   * Study Balaji to define the ICP precisely. We have one real reference customer (Scale Jobs) and the founder's traits to pattern-match against.

   * They are at 80k to 100k MRR

   * We could also look for VC funded companies that found PMF (others won’t do SEO/AEO)
2. **Agencies** — agencies that lack this capability or struggle to find/setup new resources. FDM is very useful to them: it trains their team, then they use the tool, experimentation, and results.

   * Sold as the same product, positioned differently.

   * Requires an **FDM course** to be ready

Both target ICPs share the FDM premise: the internal team has direct influence and control; it's not a complete offload, though we'll still do the heavier lifting when needed.

There are likely other ICPs to target eventually — separate problem, lower priority for now.

## Outbound

Principle: **small volume, ultra-personalized.**

### Lead sources

* People who react to our posts.

* People who visit the Agent Berlin / company page or Sherin's personal page (LinkedIn surfaces this; on Twitter/Reddit only people who interacted).

* **RB2B** for website visitor leads — worth exploring.

* **Clay** — leads pulled by defined criteria.

* **Communities** — WhatsApp groups, Discord groups, LinkedIn groups, etc. Find where the ICP already congregates and reach out there.

Every company on the list carries a **reason** they're there (visited a page, reacted to a post, found via an interesting post, or matched a Clay criterion). That reason lives in the lead record.

### Ultra-personalization

* Build a system: input a domain name → get back the SEO issues/problems (an audit) → use that as the personalization hook.

* Must check whether this is **economical**.

* Fallback "lighter" personalization: Sherin or Ananya manually builds the audit report and runs the person through the product.

### Tools

* **Instantly** — email outreach. _Pending setup._ (Only outstanding automation.)

* **Meet Alfred** — LinkedIn. Already have it.

### Process

* A single file/list of people, bucketed by ICP.

* Pick **5–10 per day** and reach out.

* Track response rate → use it to refine/narrow the ICP over time.

* **Multi-channel:** for each lead, gather phone, email, LinkedIn, and any other social, and reach out across channels.

### Sequence

* **First message must be personalized.**

* Second message can carry the heavier work (e.g., the built-out audit).

### Problems

* ICP (shared problem).

* The personalization/audit tool doesn't exist yet.
